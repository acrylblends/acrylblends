Hybrid retrieval: why both matter

A strong search system does not need to choose between lexical matching and semantic similarity. Each captures a different kind of relevance.

Dense retrieval is effective when a user describes a concept in different words. Exact matching remains important for identifiers, names and technical tokens.

The final ranking stage should combine scores, source quality and freshness with transparent explanations.
